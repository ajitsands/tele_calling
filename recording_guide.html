Architecture Overview

Centralized Call Recording to Laptop Server

All 5+ telecaller smartphones upload their recorded voice conversations directly to your laptop over the local Wi-Fi network. After verified storage, files are automatically purged from phones to guarantee zero memory exhaustion.

Laptop Server Endpoint http://192.168.8.11:8000
Laptop Storage Folder backend/storage/recordings/
Mobile Memory Protection Auto-Purge on 200 OK

1 End-to-End Call Lifecycle & Auto-Delete Mechanism

A

1. Live Call on Smartphone

The customer calls the telecaller. The laptop pops up customer history instantly. The phone dialer records the two-way conversation audio.

B

2. Wi-Fi Upload to Laptop

When you hang up, the TeleCalling Gateway detects the audio file and sends a multipart stream to /api/recordings/upload on your laptop.

C

3. Auto-Delete from Phone

Once your laptop confirms the file is safely written to disk, the app immediately executes file.delete(), leaving phone storage 100% free!

2 How to Enable Auto-Recording by Phone Model

OPPO & OnePlus (ColorOS / OxygenOS) Your Current Device
  1. Open the phone's default Phone / Dialer app.
  2. Tap the 3 dots ⋮ (Settings) in the top right corner.
  3. Tap Call recording.
  4. Turn ON 👉 "Automatically record unknown numbers".
  5. Tap "Automatically record these numbers" and enable "Always record selected numbers".
Samsung Galaxy (One UI) Samsung
  1. Open the Phone app.
  2. Tap 3 dots ⋮ > Settings.
  3. Tap Record calls.
  4. Turn ON 👉 "Auto record calls" (Choose All calls).
Xiaomi, Redmi & POCO (MIUI / HyperOS) Xiaomi
  1. Open Phone / Dialer.
  2. Tap the Gear icon ⚙️.
  3. Tap Call recording.
  4. Turn ON 👉 "Record calls automatically" (Select All numbers).
Vivo & iQOO (Funtouch OS) Vivo
  1. Open Settings > Phone > Call settings > Record settings.
  2. Select 👉 "Record all calls automatically".

3 How to Verify Recordings on Your Laptop

Once a call finishes and you hang up, open your laptop browser:

1. Open the Web Application

Go to http://192.168.8.11:3000 and click on the Call Logs & Audio tab.

2. Click the Play ▶ Button

Each completed call displays an audio player pill. Click it to listen to the recorded conversation instantly.
